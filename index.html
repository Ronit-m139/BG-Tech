Module 01 // The Issue

Why Data Collection Matters

Every time you click onto something, search, or even scroll, data is collected, and monetised. The purpose of this module is to explain how enterprise data systems work, who is collecting your data, and also how it shapes your behaviour as a consumer.

01 // The core problem

"We thought we were searching Google. It turns out, Google was searching us."

— Adapted from Shoshana Zuboff, The Age of Surveillance Capitalism, 2019

The modern day internet runs on your hidden ecnomnic exchange: You receive free services, and in return, companies get your data. This business model is known as "Surveillance capitalism", treats human behaviour as a raw material that has the potential to be harvested and sold. It is the main source of revenue for some of the world's largest companies.

For Australian Year 11 students, this is a big issue. Platforms that you use on a daily basis; Tiktok, Instagram, Google, Youtube, and spotify are enterprise computing systems designed to maximise the collection, storage, and the monetisation of your data. Understanding how these systems work is a important skill for operating in a economy that is heavily data driven.

04 // Manipulation techniques

Social Proof Loops

Like counts and follower numbers trigger social comparison, driving continued engagement and content creation to gain validation.

Algorithmic Amplification

Content that provokes strong emotional reactions spreads further. Outrage and anxiety are more "engaging" than neutral content, so algorithms surface them preferentially.

Dark Patterns

UI designs that make privacy settings hard to find, consent checkboxes pre-ticked, and "accept all" more prominent than "manage settings."

Filter Bubbles

Personalisation algorithms narrow the information you see, limiting exposure to alternative perspectives and reinforcing existing beliefs.
